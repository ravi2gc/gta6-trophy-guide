    Roadmap to the Platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies and no difficulty setting. The public guides report no mission replay, so story-moment trophies are done while the mission is live — and cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. Work the stages in order; each stage lists its trophies, and the tracker above stays in sync.

    MISSABLE can be lost if its moment passes  ·  STORY hidden by the spoiler setting until earned  ·  ×N a counted target

      Facts the roadmap is built on

      • 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver, 46 Bronze; 28 of them hidden on PlayStation until earned.
      • One playthrough is enough; free roam continues after the final mission.
      • Hardest trophy: A Long Road (100% completion) — track it in-game at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.
      • Only one trophy is reported as possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude.
      • Every guide in this app names its sources; each trophy card links them.
