Roadmap at a glance

    Fifty-five trophies, each with an own-words guide, its unlock conditions, and the sources that back every claim. Guides are never copied from a source: they are written fresh for this codex and cite the pages that were actually read on 21 November 2026. Story trophies are hidden behind the spoiler shield by default.

    Six stages to the platinum

    PowerPyx breaks the run into 60–80 hours of story, about 100 hours of 100% checklist, and about 10 hours of cleanup — roughly 200 hours in all. Push Square warns it can run past 250 hours, and one outlier puts it at 100–200. Our six stages follow PowerPyx's shape, split so the story and the checklist each get room. See Disputes for the full argument.
